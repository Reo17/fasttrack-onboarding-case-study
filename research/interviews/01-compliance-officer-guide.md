# Interview Guide: AML/CFT Compliance Officer
Version: 0.1 | Status: Draft | Author: Debesh Shankhi | Updated: 2026-11-03
Purpose: To understand the current screening process, pain points, and conditions for accepting risk-based screening (A-08).

## 1. Opening
Thanks for making the time. I’m the business analyst on the FastTrack onboarding project. Today I’d like to understand how AML/CFT screening works for new members now, what makes it hard, and what you’d need to see before any part of it changes. This should take about 30 minutes. I’ll take notes, and I’ll send you a summary afterwards so you can correct anything I’ve got wrong. Before we start, is there anything you’d like to know about the project?

## 2. Role and context
Q1. Can you tell me what your team is responsible for when a new member applies?
    Probe: How many people are on the team? What else competes for their time?
    Why: context; how much capacity screening uses.

## 3. Current process
Q2. Walk me through what happens from the moment an application reaches your team to when you clear it.
    Probe: How long does each step take? Where do applications wait?
    Why: As-Is process and the 2.8-day compliance queue.

Q3. How do you decide whether a watchlist match is genuine?
    Probe: What information do you look at? Who makes the final call?
    Why: the decision rules that will become RULE-xx.

Q4. Of the applications you screened last month, roughly how many were real matches?
    Probe: And how many looked like a match at first but weren't?
    Why: tests A-02 (85% no match) and the false-positive rate.

Q5. What records do you keep for each check, and where?
    Probe: How would you find one from three years ago?
    Why: C-04 (5-year retention) and the audit trail.

## 4. Pain points
Q6. Which part of screening takes the most of your team's time?
    Probe: Why? How often does that happen?
    Why: pain points PP-xx and bottleneck evidence.

Q7. Tell me about the last application that was difficult to screen.
    Probe: What made it hard? How was it resolved?
    Why: exception cases the new process must handle.
    
## 5. Concerns and constraints
Q8. If some applicants were cleared without a manual check, what would you need to see to feel confident in that?
    Probe: What would make you say no?
    Why: A-08, the key question of the interview.

Q9. What would you need to check, after the fact, to be sure the process was working?
    Probe: How often? Who would do it?
    Why: R-01 mitigation and the OBJ-05 monthly QA sample.

Q10. Is there anything the law or our AML/CFT programme requires that a new process must not change?
    Probe: Where is that written down?
    Why: confirms C-03 and finds missing constraints.
    
## 6. Success and close
Q11. A year from now, what would tell you this project went well?
    Why: success from their point of view, for the objectives.

Q12. Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?

Q13. Who else should I talk to?
