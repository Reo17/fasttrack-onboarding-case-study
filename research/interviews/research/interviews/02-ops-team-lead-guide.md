# Interview Guide: Ops Team Lead
Version 0.1 | Status: Draft | Author: Debesh Shankhi | Updated: 2026-11-05
Purpose: understand the current application process, pain points, and information about the excel tracker.

To know from this interview:
1. How does their team process an application step by step? (needed for As-Is process documentation)
2. Where does the 52 minutes touch time actually go? (OBJ-03)
3. How accurate is the Excel tracker? (A-07)
4. Which messy exception cases come up? (R-05)
5. How does the team feel about the change? (sensitive!) (R-05)
6. Why do applications get bounced for documents? (whats the reason for 27% bounce rate?)

## 1. Opening
Thank you for your time. I'm the business analyst on the FastTrack onboarding project. Today, I'd like to understand how the operations team handles each application step by step, what exceptions make the process messy, and how we can help the team accept this positive change. This should take about 30 minutes, I'll be taking notes, and will send you a summary afterwards so you can correct anything I might've got wrong. Before we start, is there anything you would like to know about the project?

## 2. Role and context
Q1: Firstly, could you tell me what your team is responsible for when a new membership application is made?
Probe: How many people are on your team? How many people are involved in one application? 

## 3. Current process
Q2: Could you walk me through, how are each onboarding application handled? 
Probe: How long does each step usually take? Where do the application gets stuck? Why? How is the 52minutes touch time actually being used?

Q3: How trustable are the excel tracker data do you think? Is that our only source of truth for these data points?

Q4: Of the applications you processed last month, roughly what were the common exceptions that took most of the time to handle? How easy was it to handle? How were those cases handled?

## 4. Pain points
Q5: What are those messy exceptions that keep coming up and are hard to handle by the team? And how often are those encountered?

Q6: Could you tell me how excited the team is to have those messy exceptions away for the good? Is there anything we could do that would make the team more confident towards this positive change?
