## 2. Problem statement
The problem of new member onboarding taking 9 days of which most of it is wait times affects new applicants, the onboarding team and the bank itself, the impact of which is that 34% of applicants are abandoning onboarding due to long wait times. 27% of applications were bounced for missing documents which results in wasted time for both the onboarding team and applicants. The onboarding team has spent 52mins on manual processing per application on average, which could be reduced. There has also been 4% error in re-keying into core banking which directly impacts bank's trust and data registry leading to potential issues later on.
A successful solution would reduce the median onboarding time to 1 business day or less with less than 15% abandonment rate, and reduce the manual touch time per application to 12mins or less with zero re-keying error rate.
